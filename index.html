Drop & Merge : Merge Game • Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

This page explains what data is collected when you use Drop & Merge : Merge Game, why this data is used, and how advertising-related services may process data. The game can be played without directly requesting your name, email address, phone number, or home address.

Last updated: June 3, 2026

Data We Collect

Drop & Merge : Merge Game collects limited technical and gameplay data to operate the game, save scores, show the leaderboard, improve the game experience, and display advertisements.

Data That May Be Collected
  • Device ID or a unique device identifier generated by the app.
  • Technical information such as platform, operating system version, app version, and last seen time.
  • Gameplay statistics such as score, game mode, game duration, merge count, and highest level reached.
  • Leaderboard display name and avatar color selected by the player.
  • Ad impression and ad interaction information.
  • Diagnostic data such as crashes, errors, and performance information.
How We Use Data
  • To save scores and display the leaderboard.
  • To improve game performance, balance, and user experience.
  • To show ads, measure ads, and support advertising systems.
  • To detect errors, prevent abuse, and protect app security.

Account and Identity Information

The game can be played without creating an account. If you choose to appear on the leaderboard, your selected player name may be stored together with your score records. The device ID is used to associate scores with the same device and manage duplicate or repeated records.

Advertisements

Drop & Merge : Merge Game may include third-party advertisements. Advertising services may process device identifiers, advertising IDs, ad impression data, ad interaction data, and similar technical information to deliver ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud or abuse. You can manage your advertising and tracking preferences in your device settings.

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services for scores, leaderboards, analytics, cloud infrastructure, and advertising. These services may process data according to their own privacy policies. Services used by the app include Google Mobile Ads and Supabase.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared with service providers only when necessary for game operation, leaderboards, analytics, advertising, security, or legal obligations.

Data Retention

Score and leaderboard records may be retained for as long as leaderboard and score history features are available. Technical and diagnostic data is retained only as long as necessary to improve and secure the app.

Children's Privacy

Drop & Merge : Merge Game does not knowingly target or collect direct personal information from children. If you believe such information has been collected by mistake, please contact us.

Security

We use reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect data. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

Your Rights

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your data. To evaluate your request, we may ask for information needed to identify the relevant records, such as your device ID or leaderboard display name.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be published on this page or through a new app version.
